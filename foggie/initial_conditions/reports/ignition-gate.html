The finding this plan responds to

One inequality sets the faint‑end threshold

In our decks both optional gates in star_maker_h2reg.F are disabled (NumberDensityThreshold = 0, MinimumH2Fraction = 0). The only test the code applies is the minimum‑mass one, and because t_ff ∝ nH−1/2 and m_cell ∝ nH dx³ at fixed refinement level, it collapses to a single scaling.

m_form = min(0.9, ε · fH2 · dt/τ) · m_cell  ≥  10 M☉ R  ∝  fH2 · nH3/2 · dx³ R ≥ 1 is ignition. Our dark halos sit at R = 0.019–0.092 — short by 11–52×.

Repairing a dead stochastic branch that had been discarding all sub‑threshold star formation ignites three halos that were dark in every previous configuration. Predicted yields from the measured R histories were 7 / 93 / 258 particles; measured 7 / 81 / 323. The question this document answers is whether that is a result the field needs, and what it obliges us to run next.

Physics

One advantage, two gaps

Non‑equilibrium H2

We feed the star maker Grackle's actual H2I+H2II field. LYRA takes H2 from equilibrium tables; Kuhlen+ used the KMT09 analytic model, which returns exactly zero at zero metallicity. Only EDGE2 matches us, and they add radiative transfer on top.

No Lyman‑Werner treatment

LW intensity is zero, there is no local LW and no dissociating band. LYRA's headline result is that the early LW background moves the dark‑to‑luminous transition a full dex, from 107 to 108 M☉, and that ultrafaints are exactly the population that cares. Our norad arm is the maximally optimistic LW case.

No Pop III enrichment floor

Our dark halos sit at Z = 7.7×10−9 Z☉ forever. Kuhlen+ imposed Z = 10−3 Z☉ at z=9 because their H2 model gives nothing at Z=0. The metals→dust→H2 bootstrap cannot start, by construction — a choice we did not make deliberately.

Assessment

What is genuinely new

Not novel: resolution — we are behind LYRA, EDGE2 and FIRE‑2 on baryons; H2‑regulated star formation in Enzo, which is Kuhlen+ 2012 running this same routine; and “reionization quenches ultrafaints,” settled since 2000.

  1. The minimum star particle mass is the threshold — measured, predicted, removed.

    Kuhlen+ 2012 wrote that a hard mass threshold “can lead to a significant amount of unfulfilled star formation, although this can be remedied with a stochastic SF criterion,” then used stochastic sampling. The warning is fourteen years old and nobody has measured how much star formation the hard threshold discards, or shown that it sets the apparent halo‑mass threshold. This applies to any AMR code with a coarse spatial floor and a hard minimum particle mass — it is not exotic to Enzo.

  2. A matched three‑arm radiation ladder on the same halos.

    Fiducial (Enzo's hard‑coded z=7 gate), reion (unmodified HM2012 from z=15.13), and a true zero‑radiation arm, 18 halos each on identical initial conditions. That third arm is what separated “the UVB removes the fuel” from “the halo cannot make H2 anyway.” Removing the background entirely buys only 1–10× of the 11–52× needed.

  3. Enzo's silent RadiationRedshiftOn = 7.

    Every Enzo run using tabulated HM2012 has been getting a step function at z=7 rather than the tabulated history. Worth a short methods note on its own; it is a trap for the whole user base.

The discrepancy to confront

LYRA finds ignition in progenitors of M200c ~ 105–106 M☉ at z ≳ 8. Our dark halos are at 107–108 and never ignite at all. That is one to two decades of disagreement with the newest and highest‑resolution study in the field — explained by some combination of the mass gate, the missing Pop III metals, and resolution. All three are testable.

Plan

Three tiers, in priority order

Tier A is ~12 runs and ~8 TB, branched from norad at z=12.5 and stopped at z=4. Do not queue Tier B until Tier A reports.

Tier AClose the two gaps the literature says dominatehighest value

A1 · Pop III enrichment floor. Add Z = 10−3 Z☉ at z=9 following Kuhlen+ exactly, with 10−4 and 10−2 variants. This is the standard of the lineage our prescription comes from and its absence is an unforced error. Expect the largest effect after stochastic sampling.

A2 · Lyman‑Werner background. LWbackground_intensity and TabulatedLWBackground already exist in the deck, both at zero. Run LYRA's two prescriptions so our results are directly comparable to the paper that will be the reference.

Tier BThe resolution statement, required regardlessturns the weakness into the experiment

B1 · nref9 → 10 → 11 on 543386 and 537545 with stochastic star formation on. Since R ∝ dx³, the prediction is that the deterministic threshold moves up ~0.9 dex per level while the stochastic one barely moves. If that holds it is the cleanest demonstration that stochastic sampling is the resolution‑independent choice.

B2 · the L4 rung of the nref9 dark matter sweep. 47314‑L4 reached z=0 on 09‑08; the L2 rung was deleted the same day, so this is L3 versus L4 only.

Tier CFinish the stochastic result3 runs

C1 · stochastic off the fiducial arms rather than norad, to answer whether the ignited halos re‑quench once radiation is restored. The fiducial‑R prediction says the three smallest drop to 3–6 particles formed almost entirely before z=6 — ignite‑then‑quench fossils — while the rest keep forming. That estimate cannot see the enrichment bootstrap or SN blowout, which is the whole point of running it.

C2 · a bright halo (42502 or 15659) branched the same way. The control 235659 moved from 140 to 1.7×104 M☉ — a factor of 100. If the well‑resolved end moves similarly the recipe cannot go fleet‑wide as it stands.

C3 · aperture. R200c is ~7 ckpc/h for a 2×107 M☉ halo and its star count swings with merger bookkeeping — halo174526 read 7 inside R200c against 28 in a fixed 100 ckpc/h aperture. Adopt a fixed comoving aperture for the ultrafaints and say so.

What we should not claim
  • Not a resolution record. Say plainly that EDGE2 is 3 pc and LYRA is 4 M☉, and that our threshold is a threshold at nref9.
  • Not the discovery that H2 regulates dwarf star formation — that is Kuhlen+ 2012 and Gnedin+ 2009.
  • Nothing about the Lyman‑Werner background until A2 exists.

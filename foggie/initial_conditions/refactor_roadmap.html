Automating the 25 Mpc zoom IC → Enzo pipeline

Refactor roadmap — replacing the hand-driven script512.py / script256.py workflow with a registry-driven, self-advancing pipeline.

branch: ics_refactor base: master @ e20f8bf8 plan: REFACTOR_PLAN.md

The problem

Every refinement level needs a human. Submit L1.sh, wait hours-to-days for Enzo to reach RD0265 (z = 0), notice it finished, hand-submit L2.sh, repeat. Nothing reports fleet state except tail-ing OutputLog. The cost is idle machine time between levels and silent stalls — halo15097/25Mpc_DM_512-L1 died at RD0259 on a walltime kill and simply sat there.

The pathway

Each level is two jobs: a small build job that generates ICs, and a large run job that evolves them to z = 0. The green advance hook is the piece that does not exist today — the manual gap it closes is marked in orange.

Per-stage state machine

A stage is (halo, box, level, phase). Its state is a pure function of files already on disk — no database, no bookkeeping to corrupt. That is what makes advance idempotent and safe to call from two places at once.

Two triggers, one engine

Regression: recreated halo42189 vs the preserved manual run

Expecting a clean diff everywhere would be wrong. The comparison splits into three tiers by what can legitimately match.

Tier 1 — must match exactly

MUSIC configs, shift values, CosmologySimulationGrid* geometry, final .enzo files. Deterministic text. Any difference is a bug in the rewrite, not physics — except the known fixes, which get enumerated and justified rather than waved through.

Tier 2 — expected bitwise

The MUSIC IC files. Seeded from fixed seed[5..13] and the shared wnoise_*.bin, so identical configs should give identical ICs. A mismatch means an input drifted — seed, transfer function, or the traced Lagrangian region.

Tier 3 — physical only, never bitwise

The Enzo outputs. MPI reduction order varies with decomposition, so trajectories diverge at round-off and amplify. Compare the science instead: halo center, Rvir, Mvir at RD0265, with cycle count (1208 / 1208 / 1209) as a sanity check.
